\begin{abstract}

MotoSafe GBA is a web-based cartographic information platform for
motorcyclists in the Buenos Aires Metropolitan Area. It integrates
historical motorcycle-crash records, public-safety layers, local news
and weather data while exposing source, age and spatial coverage. Its
machine-learning component estimates potential crash severity
conditional on a crash having occurred; it does not predict crash
occurrence per trip. Historical evidence and model validation are
limited to the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, while Greater Buenos
Aires layers are exploratory context. The MVP combines React and
MapLibre, FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS and a traceable direct-news
ingestion process. The report explicitly states the coverage and
deployment limits that remain pending, along with the results already
obtained and their limitations in temporal model validation and user
evaluation.

\end{abstract}
